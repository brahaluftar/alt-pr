Simple Solutions

Project Management Notification

Working daily with different projects, it can be hard to manage deadlines with many tools, and follow-up project on time. It happens sometimes that being out of the office, in a meeting, or traveling, that PM can bypass a warning.
We built, with the help of AI a simple script to process deadlines and provide a notification system by email, just from Google Sheet, developed with Google Apps Scripts.
It is easily reusable, and is completely private.
Just make a copy of the google sheet, copy your project details and deadlines and tweak a bit with few configurations provided here.Of course, a Google Account of your own is needed.

Simple Solution

Roster Automation

Lots of organization managers struggle in their routine work to create a roster for shifts of their personnel. Lots of requests, criteria, and limitations come with their monthly routine.
